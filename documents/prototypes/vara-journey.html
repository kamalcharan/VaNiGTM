PROPOSAL / JOURNEY LOGIC

Make the next action truthful.

Vara keeps its own landing and onboarding. VaNi supplies the organisation context; Vara takes the person to their first useful hiring outcome.

  1. Inherit, then proceed. Industry and company context come from Smart Profile. No repeated company questionnaire.
  2. Preparation runs alongside the task. Ready, preparing, failed and no-match states are distinct. Keep the role title while waiting. Manual drafting stays available.
  3. Role first. Match the company's own family first, then an industry playbook. A matched role opens a proposed draft; questions are reserved for gaps.
  4. Keep work safe. This prototype saves drafts in this browser. Production should save drafts to the workspace and support resuming across devices.
  5. Review before publication. Candidate-facing text and internal assessment criteria have separate review checks. Editing a job does not silently change family defaults.
  6. Name the milestone. Job published, website verified, and applications enabled are separate states. Application intake is still unavailable.
  7. Return to useful work. Continue the draft, connect the site, or add the next job. A second job demonstrates reuse; it is not required to complete initial setup.
All publication, enrichment and installation outcomes here are simulations. No API calls, credentials, applicant data or external writes.
