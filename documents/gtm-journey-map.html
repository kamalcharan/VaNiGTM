VANI GTM · TENANT JOURNEY · v1 DRAFT

Ten minutes to a list worth a message

A founder has a product and a list of companies they think should buy it. They are not here to build a CRM — they want to know which of these are worth a message, and to whom, by this afternoon. Everything VaNi already knows about them (offers, buyer, vocabulary, brand) only pays off if it arrives as decisions to confirm, not as forms to fill.

The journey — the first ten minutes

Six stations. Under each: what the tenant does, what GTM does, what the system reads or writes — and, in red, where today's build departs from it. Green means the station holds.

1

Land — “here is who you sell to”

The agent opens on what it already knows, not on a question. The Smart Profile is read, never re-asked: buyer, pain, offers, the words the market uses. One thing is named as weakest, with why it matters to GTM, and one action follows.

Tenant
Signs in, opens GTM, reads a page about themselves
GTM
Reads the profile; scores readiness; says “offers: one defined — enough to start” or “no buyer yet — define one first”
System
Reads gt_tenant_profile, gt_semantic_clusters, brand, offers. Writes nothing.
Today · this station does not exist There is no /agents/gtm. The retired console's /today was the closest thing: a Brain-completeness ring and a “your agents” strip — a landing, not a queue, and not GTM's. And PROFILE_COMPLETE is emitted when the brain crosses the line and consumed by nothing: the moment the profile becomes good enough, nothing proposes an audience.
2

Bring — where the companies come from

Three postures, one question: I have a list · I have nothing · I have my own Apollo or Clay. After this station the road is the same whichever they chose — which the playground has to prove.

Tenant
Drops a spreadsheet, or picks the pool, or pastes a provider key that never comes back
GTM
Maps the columns and shows the mapping before anything lands; says how many rows, how many with a domain, how many duplicates
System
Upload: ki_import_staging → gt_source_loads → gt_prospects. Own provider: gt_prospects only — never the pool (rule 13)
Today · one posture of three Upload works end to end — the ETL 501 is gone. But it is a 2,016-line destination (import + import-dashboard), not a step. The platform pool is schema only: gt_universe_* exists, gt_connectors does not. A tenant's own provider: nothing — no table, no key store, no puller.
3

Find — companies that match, with why

GTM proposes a cohort from what was brought, framed by the market vocabulary the tenant ratified in the wizard. Every row says why it is here. The tenant confirms, drops, tags — reacting, not searching.

Tenant
Reads the cohort; unticks the ones that are obviously wrong
GTM
Builds the cohort; shows completeness, validity, freshness per row — data that supports a yes/no, nothing decorative
System
prospect-skill.build_cohort, save_segment, tag_prospects
Today audience/find is 1,591 lines and opens on an offers form with a checklist of what is still missing. Step 1 gates step 2 with a disabled button that says why. The right instinct — a fit score against a half-written offer is a number that looks meaningful and is not — expressed as a wall. The missing thing should be the screen, and it should be the Smart Profile's screen, not a second editor.
4

Qualify — briefs, and a decision per company

The agent goes outward: for each company, what it found on their site and on the open web, scored against each offer, with the evidence linked — and the smallest ask that fits. The tenant reads a brief and decides: worth a message, or not.

Tenant
Reads; decides worth it / not / later; the verdict is remembered
GTM
Research batch with a visible budget; fit per offer blind to commitment, then opens with the entry ask; lessons proposed from what the tenant decided
System
research-skill.start_research → SearXNG → gt_account_briefs (tenant-scoped, FK to gt_prospects — a brief cannot attach to a pool row); decide_brief, propose_lessons
Today · holds, and is buried This is the manufacturing pilot and it works: 17 functions, budget, evidence, verdicts, lessons. It is the best-built part of G1 and it is invisible under the offers form in the same 1,591-line page. Two environmental facts to carry: research runs one LLM call at a time on the platform (the lane), and SEARXNG_URL was not set on the Main VPS when last checked — re-check, do not assume.
5

People — who, at each company worth it

For every “worth a message”, the decision-makers: title, LinkedIn, an email if one can be found — and an honest “no email found” when it cannot. The enrichment waterfall shows each provider tried, hit or miss.

Tenant

GTM
Finds people from the brief; tries providers in order; never invents a contact
System

Today · the backend is the most complete in the repo, the screen is a table contact-skill has 16 functions. gtm/people is a 511-line list with a 788-line detail. No waterfall exists because no provider exists: with no connector, “enrich” can only mean what was in the upload. That is fine to ship — say it, do not fake a spinner over it.

System

Story — the offer-story per segment, the move per person

The storytelling agent composes per offer and per segment. The hospital-offer story is an ASSET (about us, reused); the message that references this hospital is a MOVE (about them, never reused). That line is already in the schema, and it is the line between nurture and personalised spam.

Tenant
Reads the story; edits in their voice; approves
GTM
Writes from the profile, brand and the brief's evidence; says what it read and what it did not (the nodes it dropped for budget)

Today · built, not shown The story library exists (migration 225, five functions). No console has ever listed a story or a content kind.

Six decisions before the playground is approved

Each carries a recommendation. None is mine to take — each changes what a tenant sees first or what the schema stores.

Where does a new GTM tenant land — G1's first step, or Today?

Today is a queue of what has gone quiet. On day one nothing has gone quiet, because nothing is in motion.

Recommend The agent landing, whose journey rail says Build the audience is the current step. Today becomes the landing's current step only once something is in motion.

Is Import a pathway step or a destination?

The upload flow works and is 2,016 lines of its own screens. The pathways may absorb most of what it does.

Recommend It is station 2 in the upload posture: one step with a mapping preview. The dashboard of past imports becomes a drawer on that step, not a page.

Keep the names — Build the audience · Put them in motion · Today?

They are verbs, which is right. The playground uses them until told otherwise; renaming after the screens exist is cheap, renaming after tenants learn them is not.

Recommend Keep. Reference surfaces stay nouns: People, Journeys.

Show all of G2 with Activate locked, or hide the last step until consent exists?

A hidden step is a surprise later; a locked one with its reason is a promise the tenant can plan around.

Recommend Show it, locked, reason on the step: “nothing sends until consent and suppression exist”. Rule 12: label the degraded thing.

Show the platform-pool posture though gt_connectors does not exist?

A tenant with no list should know the option exists before choosing the one that works today — and should not be told it works when it does not.

Recommend Show all three postures; pool and own-provider labelled not yet connected with the honest line under each. Upload is the road that works.

Do GTM and Vara share gt_channels and one cadence budget?

Fatigue is a person's. A candidate who is also a prospect at a customer is one human being touched by two agents.

Recommend One governor, one budget, per tenant — the governor is contact-keyed and agent-agnostic by design. Channels shared; identity per agent decided once on the tenant flag. Open in design note §8; only station 9's copy changes either way.

What this map changes

The form stops being the first screen and becomes the exception. Every station opens on the agent's finished work — a readiness read, a mapped list, a cohort, a brief, a set of people, a ranked queue — and asks for a decision. Where the tenant has not given VaNi enough to work from, GTM says which thing is missing and links to the screen that owns it. It never grows a second editor.

And the motion layer starts being seen. The governor, the story library, the touch log and the channels go in front of a tenant read-only, before a single message is sent — so that when consent exists and the lock on station 10 comes off, the tenant already knows what the window is, what the story says, and who is owed a reply.
