Five decisions before any more code

Each carries a recommendation. None of them is mine to take — they all change what the schema stores or what the tenant is asked.

Title first, or family first?

Both screens exist and they disagree. One order has to win, and the loser becomes an escape hatch.

Recommend Title first. A manager thinks in titles; the family is Vara's filing decision. The family list becomes “not that one? here are all 11”.

Is seniority a modifier, or a pack per level?

Same behavioural signals, different bar. Right now nothing in the system knows Junior from Principal.

Recommend A modifier on one family shape — years on the top must-have, a shifted threshold. 8 families × 6 levels is 48 packs nobody will maintain.

When does a tenant's shape supersede the platform's?

On first publish, silently? Or on an explicit “make this my default”?

Recommend On first publish, but said out loud — “next Full Stack role starts from this” — so it is a visible consequence, not a surprise.

Must every tenant wait on Vikuna review?

A pack is platform-wide, so review is right. But it means a tenant who triggers a 20-minute run gets nothing from it.

Recommend Publish to the requesting tenant immediately; hold platform-wide release for review. They paid the wait; they should get the answer.

gt_events: two columns, yes or no?

started_at for a claim timeout, attempts for a retry cap. Without them a dying worker orphans its events permanently — which is what happened to run 92.

Recommend Yes. Every workaround is worse: reusing processed_at makes the column's name a lie, and attempts in JSONB is a schema change in disguise.

What this map changes

The conversation stops being the road and becomes the detour. Matched roles get a document to accept; unmatched roles get the questions. The chip-by-chip interrogation of a pack Vara already holds — fromStarter — goes away. The matcher stays: its job is choosing which draft to hand over, not which questions to ask. The JD panel stays: it is already the right surface, it is just being starved.

And the middle layer starts being read. That single change is what turns the fifth station on, and it is what “save it to his space” has meant all along.
