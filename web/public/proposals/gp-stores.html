GP Stores sells on Shopify and grows through associates who bring in customers and earn commission on what those customers buy. Shopify already handles products, orders, payments and invoices. What it cannot do is run the associate programme. That is what Vikuna builds: an associate-commission engine and one mobile app with two roles — associate and admin — and, in Phase 2, a customer-facing shell around the existing website.

One app, built in React Native (Expo), delivered for Android. Associates and GP Stores' admin use the same app with different roles. The admin role is additionally published as a web build at admin.gpstores.in for desktop use — same code, no second system. GP Stores owns every account and all data — Shopify, the database, the payment gateway, the Play Store console. Vikuna builds, documents, hands over, and supports.

Associate · screen 1

Sign in

Phase 1

Associates are created by GP Stores, never self-registered. The app has a login, not a sign-up.

  • R-LOG-1Login with mobile number and password issued by GP Stores admin. No self-signup in the app.
  • R-LOG-2Password reset by OTP to the registered mobile.
  • R-LOG-3Same login works for the admin role; the app shows admin tabs to admin users.
  • R-LOG-4Deactivated associates can sign in but see a read-only ledger of what is still owed to them; no new customers can be attributed.
  • R-LOG-5A customer who becomes an associate is the same person: one identity, two roles. Identity match on mobile/email prevents self-referral.

GP Stores provides

  • The list of first-batch associates with mobile and email.
  • An SMS route for credentials and OTP (charges borne by GP Stores; provider chosen at Week 1).

Decision needed

  • Does a deactivated associate continue to receive accrued, unpaid commission at the next cycle? (Recommended: yes.)
Associate · screen 2

Home

Phase 1

Answers the three daily questions: how much is coming to me, how far to the next slab, who should I follow up with.

  • R-HOME-1Show commission due (approved, unpaid) with the next payout date, cadence, and whether the minimum threshold is met.
  • R-HOME-2Five-state ledger strip for the selected period: accrued, provisional, approved, paid, clawback.
  • R-HOME-3Sales this period on invoice value net of discounts, with target and the next slab threshold.
  • R-HOME-4Recent customers with last order date and category; tap opens the Customers screen.
  • R-HOME-5Period selector (month, quarter, year) matching how the associate's plan is defined.
  • R-HOME-6Push notification when a new customer signs up with the associate's code, when an order is attributed, and when a payout is made.

Vikuna builds

  • Order intake from Shopify webhooks → attribution to the customer's associate → accrual on invoice net.
  • Push via Firebase Cloud Messaging in GP Stores' Firebase project.

Decision needed

  • Target per associate: set by admin per period, or derived from the plan's top slab?
Associate · screen 3

Customers

Phase 1

Every customer belongs to exactly one associate. The associate sees their own customers and nothing else.

  • R-CUS-1List of the associate's customers with join date, order count, last order and this-period invoice total; search by name or mobile; filter by activity.
  • R-CUS-2One customer → one associate, fixed at signup by the referral code entered. Signup with no code (or an invalid one) assigns the customer to GP Stores directly.
  • R-CUS-3When an associate is deactivated, their customers move to GP Stores, or to another associate chosen by admin. The move applies to future orders only; past attribution is unchanged.
  • R-CUS-4Attribution is snapshotted on each order at the time it is placed, with an assignment history kept for audit.
  • R-CUS-5Row-level security: an associate's session can only read their own customers and ledger, enforced in the database, not just the app.
  • R-CUS-6Customer personal data shown is limited to name, masked mobile, and order summaries.

Shopify side

  • Customer signup on gpstores.in carries the referral code (as the associate's discount code applied on first purchase, or a signup field added by GP Stores' Shopify developers). Vikuna specifies; Shopify developers implement on the theme.
Associate · screen 4

Ledger

Phase 1

Every rupee traceable to an order. This screen is what removes commission disputes from Pavan's phone.

  • R-LED-1Per-order lines for the selected period: order number, customer, invoice net, rate applied, commission amount, state.
  • R-LED-2States: accrued (order paid), provisional (period open, slab may change), approved (period closed), paid (payout recorded), clawback (cancellation/refund deducted).
  • R-LED-3Commission is calculated only on the actual invoice amount — net of campaign and code discounts, excluding shipping and GST — never on MRP.
  • R-LED-4Tiered slabs recomputed on cumulative period sales at period close; the screen shows the slab in effect and what changes it.
  • R-LED-5Refunds and cancellations reverse commission in three cases: before period close → accrual reversed; after close but before payout → clawback reduces the approved amount; after payout → negative balance carried into the next period(s) and netted against future payouts. No time window — a refund reverses commission whenever Shopify records it.
  • R-LED-8Exchanges follow Shopify: a return plus a new order (or a return with exchange) produces a reversal and a new accrual, so commission follows the invoice value of what the customer finally keeps. Partial refunds reverse proportionally.
  • R-LED-6Payout history: date, amount, method reference entered by admin, and the period it settles.
  • R-LED-7Payout rules per associate: cadence (monthly or quarterly) and minimum threshold (e.g. ₹500); below-threshold amounts carry forward.

Decisions needed

  • Slab semantics: retroactive (6% on the whole period once ₹1,25,000 is crossed) or marginal (6% on the excess only)?
  • Commission base: confirm excluding GST and shipping.
Associate · screen 5

Invite

Phase 1

The growth loop. Built to convert on a phone, and to keep the referral attached through signup.

  • R-INV-1The associate's referral code and link are always visible; the code doubles as a Shopify discount code so it works on the website too.
  • R-INV-2Share via the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, SMS, copy link, others) with a pre-written message in English, Telugu or Hindi.
  • R-INV-3Personal invite by entering a name and mobile; the invitee receives an SMS/WhatsApp link. No access to the phone's contact list (Google Play rejects it).
  • R-INV-4The invite link opens a fast mobile landing page on gpstores.in with one call to action — sign up — with the code pre-filled; app download is offered after signup, not before.
  • R-INV-5Pending invites listed with status; optional reminder after three days.
  • R-INV-6A customer cannot invite as an associate. A customer who wants to refer others is upgraded to associate by admin first (single-level rule).

Shopify side

  • Landing page at gpstores.in/join built on the theme by GP Stores' Shopify developers to Vikuna's specification.
Admin · screen 6

Create & manage associates

Admin role

Admin uses the same app. Shopify stays the roster of record: creating an associate here creates the Shopify customer and the referral code.

  • R-ADM-1Create associate: name, mobile (login ID), email, commission plan, payout cadence and threshold, payout method details.
  • R-ADM-2On save: Shopify customer created (or existing customer upgraded) with a distinctive tag, a unique referral discount code generated and stored against the customer, login credentials sent by SMS.
  • R-ADM-3Activate / deactivate; on deactivation, choose where the associate's customers go (GP Stores or another associate).
  • R-ADM-4Associate list with search, plan, status, customers, sales and commission this period.
  • R-ADM-5Approve a customer's upgrade to associate; the system blocks self-referral and keeps the upgraded person's future purchases outside any other associate's commission.
  • R-ADM-6Admin role available on Android and as a web build at admin.gpstores.in (DNS record added by GP Stores).

Shopify side

  • Custom app with Admin API scopes for customers, discounts, orders; created by GP Stores' Shopify developers, credentials handed to Vikuna.
Admin · screen 7

Commission plans, period close & payouts

Admin role

The rules live here, as data. Changing a slab is an admin action, not a code change.

  • R-PLN-1Commission plans defined by: period type (monthly, quarterly, yearly), category scope (all or specific Shopify collections), tiered slabs on cumulative period sales, and an optional annual turnover bonus.
  • R-PLN-2Plans assigned per associate with effective dates; different associates may be on different plans and cadences.
  • R-PLN-3Period close is a human action: admin reviews and closes the period; the system then recomputes slabs on final period sales, converts provisional to approved, applies clawbacks, and produces the payout list against each associate's threshold. Approval is never automatic.
  • R-PLN-4Payouts are recorded, not executed: admin pays by bank/UPI outside the system and enters the reference; the ledger moves to paid (see screen 9).
  • R-PLN-5Dashboard: sales, signups, commission accrued/approved/paid, target vs achieved per associate and overall.
  • R-PLN-6Export to Excel and PDF: associates, ledger by period, payouts, customers by associate.
  • R-PLN-7Audit log of who changed which plan, assignment, or payout, and when.

GP Stores provides

  • The initial plan(s) in writing with worked examples, signed off in Week 1.

Decision needed

  • Annual bonus: paid at year end on the year's turnover, or accrued monthly against a running total?
Admin · screen 8

Plan builder

Admin role

Pavan changes a rate himself, sees what it pays before saving, and never rewrites history.

  • R-BLD-1Create a plan with: name, period type (monthly / quarterly / yearly), category scope (all, or selected Shopify collections), any number of slab rows (from, to, rate), and an optional annual turnover bonus (threshold, rate).
  • R-BLD-2Slab mode chosen per plan: retroactive (the reached slab's rate applies to the whole period) or marginal (each slab's rate applies only to sales within it).
  • R-BLD-3Worked-example panel recalculates live as slabs are edited, using sample months, so the effect of a change is visible before saving.
  • R-BLD-4Plans are versioned: an edit creates a new version with an effective-from date. Closed periods keep the version they were computed with.
  • R-BLD-5Clone an existing plan as the starting point for a new one; assign a plan to one, several, or all associates from the same screen.
  • R-BLD-6Validation: slabs must be contiguous and non-overlapping; a plan cannot take effect in an already-closed period.

Principle

The system calculates; a person approves; a person pays. Accrual on every paid order is automatic. Nothing becomes payable until an admin closes the period, and nothing is marked paid until an admin records the payment.
Admin · screen 9

Payout statement & record

Admin role

Money moves outside the app. The app produces the statement and keeps the record.

  • R-PAY-1Per associate per period: approved commission, clawbacks, carried-forward balance → gross payable; then statutory lines → net to pay.
  • R-PAY-2Statutory lines are displayed and recorded, not filed: TDS on commission (section 194H) at a rate and annual threshold configured by GP Stores' accountant, applied only when the associate's PAN is on file; GST added only for associates flagged as GST-registered, who then invoice GP Stores. Rates and thresholds are settings, not code.
  • R-PAY-3Payment is made by GP Stores outside the system (bank transfer / UPI). Admin records method, date, reference/UTR and net amount; may attach the associate's invoice. No payment gateway, bank API, UPI integration, TDS return or GST filing is in scope.
  • R-PAY-4"Hold" keeps a payout out of the current run with a reason (dispute, suspected self-purchase, refund pending); held amounts carry to the next run.
  • R-PAY-5On "mark as paid" the associate receives a push notification and a PDF statement; the ledger lines move to paid.
  • R-PAY-6Exports for the accountant: payout register by period with PAN, gross, TDS, GST, net and reference; per-associate annual statement.
  • R-PAY-7Below-threshold amounts (e.g. under ₹500) carry forward automatically; negative balances from post-payout refunds also carry forward and reduce the next payout.

GP Stores provides

  • Associate PAN and GST registration status at onboarding; TDS rate and threshold from its accountant. The figures on screen are illustrative — Vikuna is not advising on tax.

Requirements beyond the screens

Apply across Phase 1

Platform & ownership

  • R-SYS-1App built in React Native (Expo); Android build delivered as APK/AAB; admin web build from the same code.
  • R-SYS-2Backend on Supabase (Postgres, row-level security, edge functions) in a project owned by GP Stores; Vikuna added as collaborator.
  • R-SYS-3Shopify remains the system of record for products, customers, orders, payments and invoices. The app never calls Shopify directly; only the backend holds Shopify credentials.
  • R-SYS-4All accounts — Shopify custom app, Supabase, Firebase, SMS provider, Play Console, domain — created in GP Stores' name.

Data, integrity & audit

  • R-DAT-1Every Shopify order event (paid, cancelled, refunded) is stored raw before processing, so commissions can be recomputed and disputes traced to source.
  • R-DAT-2Commission calculations are idempotent: replaying a webhook never double-counts.
  • R-DAT-3Daily external keep-alive for the free-tier database; weekly database dump to GP Stores' Google Drive; documented trigger for moving to a paid tier.
  • R-DAT-4Audit trail on plans, assignments, reassignment of customers, period close and payouts.

Notifications & language

  • R-NOT-1Push notifications via Firebase to associates for new signup, attributed order, period closed, payout made; to admin for period-close due and refunds pending.
  • R-NOT-2Admin can send a broadcast message to all or selected associates.
  • R-NOT-3Phase 1 UI in English; the app is built with a language layer so Telugu and Hindi are added in Phase 2 without rework. Invite messages are trilingual from Phase 1.

Programme constraints

  • R-PRG-1Single-level hierarchy only. No commission on another associate's sales, no commission for recruiting associates or customers.
  • R-PRG-2Commission is only ever on invoiced, paid sales of goods; refunds and exchanges reverse it whenever they occur.
  • R-PRG-3Nothing in the app is gated behind a payment; the ₹99 (Phase 2) is store credit for goods, not an activation fee.
  • R-PRG-4No campaign management in scope; Phase 2 surfaces Shopify's promotions inside the customer shell.

Finance boundary

  • R-FIN-1The system calculates, a person approves, a person pays. No automatic approval, no automatic payment.
  • R-FIN-2All outward payments, associate invoices, TDS deposits and returns, and GST filings are handled by GP Stores and its accountant outside the system. The app records references and produces statements and registers.
  • R-FIN-3Statutory rates and thresholds (TDS on commission, GST treatment for registered associates) are configurable settings supplied by GP Stores' accountant; Vikuna does not advise on tax.
  • R-FIN-4No integration with any bank, UPI provider, payment gateway or accounting software in Phase 1 or Phase 2.

Google Play & distribution

A separate track that Google controls

What Vikuna delivers

A signed, store-ready Android build, listing text and screenshots, closed-test setup and the first submission. Vikuna assists with reviewer questions.

What Vikuna does not control

Approval, review duration, policy outcomes and re-submissions sit with Google. They are outside the fixed fee, the timeline and the warranty. Re-submission effort after the first is drawn from maintenance hours.

Account ownership

The Play Console developer account is registered by GP Stores, under its own Google identity, with its own verification. Vikuna is added as a collaborator — never the owner. The same rule applies to Shopify, Supabase, Firebase and the payment gateway.

Closed testing before production

Google currently requires new individual developer accounts to run a closed test with a minimum number of testers continuously for 14 days before production access is granted (12 testers today; GP Stores should plan for 14–15 across 15 days). GP Stores recruits and manages the testers — the first batch of associates are the natural choice. An organisation account (needs a D-U-N-S number) may bypass this but takes its own verification time; both routes are presented for Pavan's choice.

The app works without the store

Go-live never waits for Google. The Android build is shared directly (Drive link / WhatsApp) to associates and admin from day one; admin also has the web build. Direct installs need "unknown sources" enabled and receive no automatic updates; the runbook covers how a new version is pushed.

Google Play billing. GP Stores sells physical goods, so Shopify checkout inside the app is permitted. The ₹99 is implemented as an optional prepaid wallet top-up bought on gpstores.in and redeemable only against goods — never as a fee to unlock the app. The app must not restrict any feature behind that payment, and the listing will describe it as a promotion, not an activation.

iOS / App Store is out of scope for both phases.
