How We Design Training

Workflow-first.
Not theory-first.

Generic training teaches concepts and hopes people figure out the application. We start from the other end — with how your people actually work today — and design learning that slots into that reality.

Every programme we deliver is customised. We interview your teams, understand your tools, and map your actual workflows before we design a single slide.

The CDO / CAiO Connection

When Vikuna is engaged as your Fractional CDO or CAiO, training is designed in parallel with the transformation roadmap — not added on at the end. The people who will operate the new systems are trained as those systems are being designed. Capability and strategy develop together.

01

Workflow Discovery

We interview your teams and observe how work actually happens — not how it's documented. Training is designed around real tasks, real tools, real friction points.

02

Audience Mapping

Different roles need different outcomes. We map every learner group to the specific capabilities they need — not one message for the whole organisation.

03

Practical Programme Design

Sessions are built around doing, not listening. Real scenarios from your industry, real tools your team uses, real decisions they'll face on Monday morning.

04

Adoption Measurement

We don't measure attendance. We track behaviour change — are people using new skills? Has the workflow changed? Is AI actually being used for more than spell check?

05

Iterate & Reinforce

Learning doesn't end when the session ends. We build in reinforcement touchpoints — check-ins, coaching, refreshers — until the new behaviour is habitual.
